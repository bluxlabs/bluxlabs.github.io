The Ultracortex dry electrodes are specifically designed for the OpenBCI Ultracortex headset. Each electrode uses a high-quality **Ag/AgCl tetrode** mounted on a spring-buffered PETG structure, delivering stable scalp contact even during head movement.

![Electrode installation into the Ultracortex frame](/assets/products/ultracortex_electrode/electrode_install.jpg)

The modular design allows quick, tool-free replacement. Simply press the blue electrode holder into the frame — the built-in spring mechanism maintains consistent pressure without the need for conductive gel. All connections use standard **2.54 mm Dupont jumper wires**, making maintenance and customization straightforward.

This electrode system is ideal for long-term dry EEG recordings in research, BCI development, and neuroscience experiments.
